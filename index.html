Why Choose a Certified Used Car Over a Standard Used Car?

The main difference between a standard used vehicle and a certified used car usually comes down to the inspection and warranty process behind it. A typical pre-owned car dealership may sell vehicles as-is, with limited information about their condition or history. A CPO program, on the other hand, generally requires a more thorough review before a vehicle can be listed, along with some form of added warranty coverage. That extra layer of scrutiny is a big part of why buyers are often willing to pay a bit more for a certified vehicle — the peace of mind can be worth it, especially for a major purchase like a car.
